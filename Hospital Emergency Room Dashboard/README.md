# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on building an **interactive Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard** using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze patient activity, waiting time, admissions, referrals, demographics, and patient satisfaction.

The dashboard consolidates important emergency room metrics into an interactive view, making it easier to understand **patient trends, operational patterns, waiting times, and healthcare service performance**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To help healthcare teams:

- Monitor emergency room patient activity
- Analyze patient waiting time
- Understand admission and referral patterns
- Analyze patient demographics
- Monitor patient satisfaction
- Identify busy days and hours
- Support data-driven healthcare decisions

This is achieved by creating an **interactive Power BI dashboard** with multiple analytical views.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks important emergency room metrics such as:

- **Total Patients**
- **Average Waiting Time**
- **Patient Satisfaction**
- **Admitted Patients**
- **Referred Patients**
- **Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis & Visuals

### 🔹 Monthly Analysis

The Monthly View provides insights into emergency room activity across different months.

- Monthly patient volume
- Patient satisfaction trends
- Average waiting time
- Admission trends
- Referral trends
- Patient demographics

### 🔹 Consolidated Analysis

The Consolidated View provides an overall picture of emergency room performance.

- Total patient activity
- Admission vs. non-admission
- Waiting time analysis
- Patient satisfaction
- Department referrals
- Demographic distribution

### 🔹 Patient Details

The Patient Details page provides detailed patient-level information, including:

- Patient ID
- Patient Name
- Age
- Gender
- Race
- Admission Status
- Waiting Time
- Department Referral
- Admission Date

### 🔹 Time & Demographic Analysis

The dashboard analyzes patient activity based on:

- Age groups
- Gender
- Race
- Day of the week
- Hour of the day
- Department referrals

## 💡 Key Insights

- Identified patterns in emergency room patient volume.

- Evaluated **average patient waiting time**.
- Compared admission and non-admission patterns.

- Examined patient demographics.
