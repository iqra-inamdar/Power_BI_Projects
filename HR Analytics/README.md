
# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard 

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on building an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** using **Power BI** to help organizations **improve employee performance and reduce attrition**.
The dashboard provides data-driven insights into employee demographics, job roles, salary structure, tenure, and attrition patterns, enabling HR teams and management to make informed strategic decisions.

The dashboard consolidates key HR metrics into a single view, making it easier to **identify risk areas, retention challenges, and workforce trends**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To help an organization:

* Improve employee performance
* Increase employee retention
* Reduce attrition rate
* Enable data-driven HR decision-making

This is achieved by creating a **comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard** that visualizes critical workforce KPIs.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following high-impact HR metrics:

* **Total Employees:** 1.47K
* **Total Attrition:** 237
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
* **Average Age:** 37 years
* **Average Salary:** 6.5K
* **Average Years at Company:** 7.0

(As visualized in the dashboard overview) 

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis & Visuals

The dashboard provides deep insights through multiple interactive visualizations:

### 🔹 Attrition by Education

* Highest attrition observed among employees from **Life Sciences (38%)** and **Medical (27%)** backgrounds.
* Helps HR understand which educational segments need better engagement strategies.

### 🔹 Attrition by Age Group

* Maximum attrition occurs in the **26–35 age group**, indicating higher job switching in early to mid-career stages.
* Useful for designing retention programs for young professionals.

### 🔹 Attrition by Job Role

* Roles with highest attrition:

  * Laboratory Technician
  * Sales Executive
  * Research Scientist
* Enables targeted role-specific HR interventions.

### 🔹 Attrition by Salary Slab

* Majority attrition occurs in employees earning **up to 5K salary slab**.
* Indicates compensation as a major attrition driver.

### 🔹 Attrition by Years at Company

* High attrition seen in employees with **0–3 years of experience**.
* Highlights onboarding and early engagement gaps.

### 🔹 Attrition by Gender

* Attrition is higher among **male employees** compared to female employees.
* Supports diversity and inclusion analysis.
---

## 💡 Business Insights & Impact

* Identified **high-risk employee segments** based on age, salary, job role, and tenure.
* Helped HR teams focus on **early-career retention strategies**.
* Highlighted compensation-driven attrition patterns.
* Improved visibility into workforce structure and performance trends.
* Enabled leadership to take **proactive, data-backed HR decisions**.

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard successfully demonstrates how data-driven insights can be used to understand employee behavior, identify attrition patterns, and support strategic HR decision-making.
By analyzing key factors such as age, job role, education, salary, gender, and years at the company, the dashboard highlights critical areas contributing to employee attrition.

This project enables HR teams and management to proactively address retention challenges, improve workforce planning, and design targeted employee engagement strategies. 
The interactive Power BI visuals make complex HR data easy to interpret, allowing stakeholders to monitor performance trends and take timely actions.

Overall, this dashboard proves the value of analytics in transforming traditional HR processes into a more efficient, insight-driven, and strategic function, ultimately helping organizations enhance employee 
satisfaction and reduce attrition.
